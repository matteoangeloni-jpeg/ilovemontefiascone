Cantinone Food Week in Montefiascone 2026: menu and dates

From August 3 to 8, 2026, every evening from 7:30 PM, Via Trento hosts the 41st edition of the Cantinone, the food week organised by the Circolo Mf 80 under the patronage of the Municipality of Montefiascone. A different local-cuisine menu every day, a short walk from Rocca dei Papi and Santa Margherita Cathedral.

The evening menu, night by night

The Cantinone offers a different set menu every evening, from the ragù-based fettuccine of the opening night to the grand finale on Saturday. Here is the full programme as listed on the Circolo Mf 80's official poster:

Monday, August 3

Mixed bruschette · Fettuccine with ragù (meat sauce) · Tripe and beans · Chicken with peppers · Mixed salad · Dessert

Tuesday, August 4 · game night

Crostini al salmì (game pâté toasts) · Pappardelle with wild boar ragù · Wild boar "al buione" stew · Quail "alla leccarda" · Mixed salad · Fries · Dessert

Wednesday, August 5

Panzanella (Tuscan bread salad) · Ombrichelli all'amatriciana (hand-rolled pasta, tomato and guanciale) · Coda alla vaccinara (Roman-style oxtail) · Beans with pork rind · Mixed salad · Fries · Dessert

Thursday, August 6

Cantinone-style crostini · Gnocchi with tomato sauce · Porchetta-style meat roll · "My way" stew · Mixed salad · Fries · Dessert

Friday, August 7 · seafood night

Seafood salad · Scialatielli "mare e monti" (seafood and mushroom pasta) · Roasted salt cod · Octopus and potatoes · Fries · Dessert

Saturday, August 8 · grand finale

Beans in green sauce · Cantinone-style fettuccine · Pork shank · Trippa alla romana (Roman-style tripe) · Wild-greens salad · Fries · Dessert

What the Cantinone is, and why it has reached its 41st edition

The Cantinone is one of Montefiascone's longest-running food traditions: in 2026 the Circolo Mf 80 reaches its 41st edition, a mark of continuity that few other summer events in town can match. For a full week, Via Trento turns into an open-air dining room, with the club's own cooks serving a different menu every night.

It isn't a one-off tied to a single weekend: it's a fixed date on the local summer calendar, built for visitors who want to taste home-style cooking without restaurant formality, in a convivial setting a short walk from the town's main monuments.

Where Via Trento is

Via Trento sits in Montefiascone's historic centre, within easy walking distance of both the Rocca dei Papi and Santa Margherita Cathedral. That makes the Cantinone easy to pair with an afternoon walk through the town's main monuments before sitting down for dinner.

Why booking ahead helps

These are open-air dinners with limited seating, especially on themed nights such as the game evening (Tuesday, August 4) or the seafood evening (Friday, August 7). It's advisable to book ahead by calling +39 338 4373689, the number listed on the Circolo Mf 80's official poster.

Practical tips

  • Booking: call +39 338 4373689, especially for themed nights.
  • Parking: check the Montefiascone parking guide if you're driving.
  • Before dinner: pair it with a visit to Rocca dei Papi or Santa Margherita Cathedral.
  • Programme: check the Circolo Mf 80's official channels for any last-minute changes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Cantinone in Montefiascone?

The Cantinone is the food week organised by the Circolo Mf 80 in Montefiascone, reaching its 41st edition in 2026: every evening, Via Trento hosts a set-menu dinner built around local dishes.

When is the Cantinone 2026?

From August 3 to 8, 2026, every evening from 7:30 PM, in Via Trento, Montefiascone.

Where is the Cantinone held?

In Via Trento, in Montefiascone's historic centre, a short walk from Rocca dei Papi and Santa Margherita Cathedral.

How do I book a table?

By calling +39 338 4373689, the number listed on the Circolo Mf 80's official poster.

Does the menu change every night?

Yes. Each evening has its own menu, from ragù-based first courses and a game-themed night to a seafood evening on Friday, August 7, and the grand finale on Saturday, August 8.

Is the Cantinone the same event as the Wine Fair?

No. The Cantinone is a separate event organised by the Circolo Mf 80 under the patronage of the Municipality of Montefiascone. Its dates partly overlap with the Wine Fair's calendar, but it has its own venue, programme and menu in Via Trento.
