Madonna della Valle Feast 2026

From 5 to 8 September, Parrocchia San Giuseppe organises the Madonna della Valle feast: a charity card tournament, a walking pilgrimage, the reopening of the renovated church, Holy Mass and countryside games in the small valley south of town.

Confirmed information

  • Period: Saturday 5, Sunday 6 and Tuesday 8 September 2026.
  • Source used: the poster released by Parrocchia San Giuseppe in Montefiascone.
  • Method: this page only reports what appears on the poster, with no unverified additions.

2026 feast status and how to use this page

The 2026 edition runs from Saturday 5 to Tuesday 8 September, with the heart of the celebration on Sunday 6, dedicated this year also to the care of creation. This page remains useful to understand the structure, location and main moments of the tradition, and will be updated when official information becomes available for future editions.

If you are planning a visit to Montefiascone during this period, use this page together with the events in Montefiascone hub.

Saturday 5 September: the card tournament

  • 3:00 pm: briscola (Italian card game) tournament in the area in front of the church, in aid of the Chiesa della Madonna della Valle.
  • Entry: €20 per pair, from 9:00 am at the church. If the minimum number of pairs is not reached, eliminated pairs may re-enter by paying an additional fee.
  • Prizes: down to 8th place (1st two prosciutti, 2nd two spalle cured meats, 3rd two capocolli, 4th two caciotta cheeses).
  • Info and booking: 328 2719857 - 327 0017275.

Sunday 6 September: the heart of the feast

The poster describes Sunday 6 as a day "dedicated to the care of creation".

  • 8:00 am: gathering in Piazza Sant'Agostino for the walking pilgrimage to the Madonna della Valle.
  • 10:00 am: inauguration and blessing of the renovated church, accompanied by the Corale Santa Margherita choir; Holy Mass celebrated by Mons. Fabio Fabene; followed by the traditional refreshments offered by the Comitato.
  • 4:00 pm: countryside games for children.
  • 5:30 pm: Holy Rosary and blessing.
  • All day: the fountain flows with Est! Est!! Est!!! wine, with panini and porchetta.

Tuesday 8 September: Nativity of the Blessed Virgin Mary

  • 5:30 pm: Holy Rosary and Mass, celebrated by don Giuseppe Trapè.

8 September is the liturgical feast of the Nativity of Mary, to whom the Madonna della Valle church is dedicated: until the early 1900s this was the only day of the year on which the church was officiated.

The church and the history of the site

The Madonna della Valle church stands in a small valley south of Montefiascone's historic centre, along the route of the traditional walking pilgrimage that starts from Piazza Sant'Agostino. Its origins are medieval: a document from 1143 records a donation by the Bishop of Tuscania, Rodolfo, to the church's rector, Don Benedetto, of land belonging to the monastery of San Pietro in the valley. The property attached to the building later passed to the Cathedral Chapter when it was established in 1369.

For centuries the church was officiated only once a year, on the feast of the Nativity of Mary on 8 September. In the early 1900s it was restored by members of the Confraternita di Santa Maria della Valle, still active today. As an organised popular feast in its current form, with the walking pilgrimage alongside the liturgical moments, the tradition has continued for decades: in 2018 it marked the fiftieth anniversary of the restoration that reopened the chapel to the community.

Why this page is useful for visitors to Montefiascone

The Madonna della Valle feast is not designed for tourists, and that is exactly why it is one of the moments when the town shows most clearly the bond between popular faith and rural life: the walking pilgrimage to a chapel surrounded by countryside, the community refreshments, the wine fountain and the countryside games describe a Montefiascone quite different from its larger civic festivals.

For a visitor passing through during this time of year, adding a stop at the Madonna della Valle to a visit of the historic centre helps read the town through its more authentic, less touristic side.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Madonna della Valle Feast 2026?

The confirmed programme on the poster covers Saturday 5, Sunday 6 and Tuesday 8 September 2026, with the main day on Sunday 6.

Where is the Madonna della Valle?

In a small valley south of the historic centre, reached on foot via the pilgrimage that starts from Piazza Sant'Agostino on Sunday morning.

How long has it been celebrated?

The church has medieval origins (12th century); the popular feast in its current form has continued for decades, with the fiftieth anniversary of the restoration marked in 2018.
